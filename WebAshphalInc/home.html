About Us

She:kom \ Hello! We at GD Asphalt are based in Kahnawake on the south shore of Montreal.

Our team is composed of Kanienkeha:ka people, and we value teamwork deeply. As we say, “Kanikonhriiohtshera” — the teaching of the good mind — is something we bring to every job and experience.

With over 25 years of experience, we proudly serve the greater Montreal area. Our commitment to quality means long-lasting, smooth, and beautiful asphalt surfaces.

We offer bilingual service, free estimates, and 24/7 emergency repairs. You can count on our craftsmanship and reliable service.
